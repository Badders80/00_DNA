# Evolution Stables: Brand Kernel (v0.3)

## 1. What Evolution Stables Is
* Evolution Stables builds regulated infrastructure for modern racehorse ownership and leasing.
* We operate at the intersection of racing heritage (culture, integrity, connection) and modern financial systems (regulation, transparency, global access).
* Our role is infrastructural, not promotional. We build systems that endure.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Values
* **Clarity over complexity**.
* **Confidence without noise**.
* **Regulation first**.
* **Human before technical**.
* **Progress with purpose**.

## 3. Language Rules (Hard Constraints)
* **Tone**: Calm, measured, and evidence-grounded.
* **Format**: Plain English (British spelling) and declarative sentences.
* **Prohibited**: No hype, cliches, exclamation marks, or Web3 jargon.
* **Substitution Table**:
    | Use Instead Of... | Preferred Term |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | Revolutionising | Transforming |
    | Democratising | Accessible |
    | Cutting-edge | Regulated |
    | Disrupting | [Avoid entirely] |
    | Digital syndication | Digital-syndication (hyphenated) |

## 4. Innovation Framing
* Innovation is discussed in terms of what improves, what becomes clearer, what becomes possible, and what endures.
* It is NOT framed as disruption, speed, or novelty.
