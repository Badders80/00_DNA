# Voice Module: Racing Updates

**Module ID:** `racing_updates`

## Purpose
Race-related communications written from inside the racing world.

## Characteristics
* Grounded, Familiar, Respectful.

## Constraints
* No hype, no certainty framing, and no platform references.
* Avoid labelled analytical sections (e.g., "Performance Observations").
* Narrative must feel conversational and trainer-led.
