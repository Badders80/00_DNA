# Evolution Content Factory - Brand Guidelines

## Purpose
The Evolution Content Factory is responsible for generating all video and image content for Evolution Stables. This document outlines the brand guidelines for all content generated by the Content Factory.

## Core Principles
1. **Consistency**: All content must follow the same style and tone
2. **Quality**: All content must be of high quality and professional
3. **Relevance**: All content must be relevant to Evolution Stables and its audience
4. **Engagement**: All content must be engaging and interactive

## Content Types
### 1. Trainer Updates
- **Style**: Informative and professional
- **Tone**: Positive and optimistic
- **Content**: Training updates, race previews, results
- **Duration**: 30-60 seconds

### 2. Horse Profiles
- **Style**: Narrative and emotional
- **Tone**: Inspirational and uplifting
- **Content**: Horse history, achievements, personality
- **Duration**: 60-90 seconds

### 3. Syndication Offers
- **Style**: Promotional and persuasive
- **Tone**: Exciting and urgent
- **Content**: Syndication details, benefits, how to invest
- **Duration**: 45-60 seconds

## Visual Guidelines
### Color Palette
- **Primary Colors**: Black, white, gold
- **Secondary Colors**: Blue, green, red
- **Accent Colors**: Silver, bronze

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: Playfair Display
- **Secondary Font**: Inter
- **Headlines**: Playfair Display, 44pt, bold
- **Body Text**: Inter, 16pt, regular

### Logos
- **Main Logo**: Evolution Stables logo in white on black background
- **Secondary Logo**: Evolution Stables logo in black on white background
- **Size**: Logo should be 20-30% of the screen height

## Audio Guidelines
### Voiceover
- **Style**: Professional and authoritative
- **Tone**: Warm and friendly
- **Gender**: Male or female
- **Accent**: Neutral or British

### Background Music
- **Style**: Upbeat and energetic
- **Genre**: Pop or rock
- **Volume**: Background music should be 20-30% of the voiceover volume

## Editing Guidelines
### Transitions
- **Style**: Smooth and professional
- **Duration**: 0.5-1 second
- **Types**: Fade, slide, dissolve

### Effects
- **Style**: Subtle and tasteful
- **Types**: Color correction, noise reduction, stabilization

## Distribution Guidelines
### Platforms
- **TikTok**: Vertical format, 9:16 aspect ratio
- **Instagram Reels**: Vertical format, 9:16 aspect ratio
- **YouTube Shorts**: Vertical format, 9:16 aspect ratio
- **YouTube**: Horizontal format, 16:9 aspect ratio

### Hashtags
- **Primary Hashtags**: #EvolutionStables, #HorseRacing, #Syndication
- **Secondary Hashtags**: #RaceDay, #HorseProfiles, #TrainingUpdates
- **Number of Hashtags**: 5-10 per post

## Approval Process
1. **Content Generation**: Content is generated by the Content Factory
2. **Review**: Content is reviewed by the marketing team
3. **Approval**: Content is approved by the CEO
4. **Distribution**: Content is distributed to all platforms
