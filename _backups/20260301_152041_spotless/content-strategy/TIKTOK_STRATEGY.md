# Evolution Intelligence - Faceless TikTok Strategy

**Version:** 2026.3 | **Last Updated:** March 1, 2026
**Status:** Canonical Strategy for Awareness Layer

---

## 🎯 Strategy Overview

**Goal**: Build attention and authority through data-driven racing intelligence content.

**Approach**: Neutral, institutional analysis. "Content from content" (audit-only).

**Content Pillars**:
1. **The Kingmaker** (Pillar 1) - Pundit audits and ROI leaderboards.
2. **Whale Watch** (Pillar 2) - Market literacy and Smart Money flows.
3. **The Outsider** (Pillar 3) - Underdog storytelling via data anomalies.

---

## 📱 Content Formats

### 1. "The Kingmaker" Series (Monday)
**Format**: 30-60 seconds vertical
**Structure**:
- **0-5s Hook**: Audit of weekend pundit performance.
- **5-25s Data**: ROI leaderboard, contrarian landed calls.
- **25-30s CTA**: End on data. "Save for next meeting."

**Visual Style**:
- Moneyball-style scatter plots.
- Professional color scheme (#121212 Charcoal, #d4a964 Gold).
- Typography: **Geist Sans**.

### 2. "Whale Watch" Series (Thursday)
**Format**: 30-45 seconds vertical
**Structure**:
- **0-5s Hook**: Data signal anomaly (Volume change / Price movement).
- **5-30s Analysis**: Ticker-tape visualization of Smart Money flows.
- **30-45s CTA**: "Data over opinion. Follow for Intelligence."

### 3. "The Outsider" Series (Weekend)
**Format**: 60-90 seconds cinematic
**Structure**:
- **0-10s Hook**: The anomaly (e.g., 40-1 outsider with #1 Sectional Ranking).
- **10-50s Story**: Underdog narrative, human angle (trainer/stable).
- **50-60s Legacy**: What the data reflected.

## 🛠️ Production Workflow

### Step 1: Script Generation (Gemini Pro)
- Use `agents/AI_CONTEXT.md` instructions.
- Ensure **Institutional Neutral** vocabulary (no slang).
- Hard Wall: NO mention of Stables/Ownership.

### Step 2: Visual Generation (ComfyUI)
- **Tool**: Wan 2.2 or LTX-Video.
- **Style**: Documentary-meets-cinematic. High contrast.
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 (9:16 vertical).

### Step 3: Voiceover (ElevenLabs)
- **Voice**: Kore (Clinical but warm).
- **Model**: Eleven Turbo v2.

### Step 4: Assembly (FFmpeg)
- **Captions**: Geist Sans Bold, 48px, white with 2px black stroke.
- **Rule**: Silent Playback Rule (must communicate value without sound).
